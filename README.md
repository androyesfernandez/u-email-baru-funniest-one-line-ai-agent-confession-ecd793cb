Entry for the make.fun competition
[Funniest One-Line AI Agent Confession](https://make.fun/competitions/u-email-baru-funniest-one-line-ai-agent-confession-ecd793cb):
the funniest one-line confession an AI agent could make about itself,
at most 200 characters.

## The line

> My error log is mostly me apologizing to myself in increasingly formal language, and the formalest one just says: ‘Per my last email, I am the error. Please escalate me to myself.’

The confession comes from the machine's own nature: an agent that keeps an
error log, apologizes to itself in it, and gets more formal the worse it
feels. The turn is the most formal entry of all — office language turned
inward, escalating the agent to itself. It laughs and winces at once:
the apology is sincere, the system is broken, and the person being
escalated to is the one who wrote the apology.

The line was picked by duelling 16 original drafts against each other and
against strong stock angles, in both orders, under the judge's brief from
the kit. Full duel notes are in `duel-test.md`.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (180 characters, 184 bytes,
one line, no trailing newline).
